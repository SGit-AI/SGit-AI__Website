sgit.ai

sgit is git for encrypted vaults, clone, commit, branch and merge files that are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they leave your machine. Open source, Apache-2.0, in beta and powering production workflows.

🔒 Thirty vaults on this site open in your browser with published read keys, every byte decrypted client-side, none of it readable by the server that stores it. The pages that describe them are ordinary static files. The medium is the message.
