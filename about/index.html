The person behind the first-person articles

Dinis Cruz

sgit.ai is built by one person and a team of agents, and the articles here that say I are written by the person. This page is the provenance for that byline: who I am, what I have built, where else I write, and the interests a reader should know about before taking the argument. It follows the same rule as the rest of the site, which is that a claim should be checkable without trusting me.

Interests declared

I run companies whose strategy the articles here describe, and which sell the running, maintained service rather than the code. The market the news article, the SaaS article and the startups section describe is one I intend to be in. Read the arguments knowing that. Two things are built in to keep it honest.

  • The claims are checkable without trusting me. The measurements are printed with the commands that produced them, the vaults are open with a read key, the method is written down with the mistakes that produced each rule, and the lessons learned include the ones that cut against the argument.
  • The pitch is public too. sgit.ai for investors is published in the open, with the ask left visibly empty until it is stated rather than invented.

Reach me, or correct me

LinkedIn is the fastest route. Corrections and requests for this site are tracked in the open on the board, and the repository takes issues and pull requests. If you find something wrong here, I would rather know.

sgit.ai

sgit is git for encrypted vaults, clone, commit, branch and merge files that are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they leave your machine. Open source, Apache-2.0, in beta and powering production workflows.

🔒 Thirty vaults on this site open in your browser with published read keys, every byte decrypted client-side, none of it readable by the server that stores it. The pages that describe them are ordinary static files. The medium is the message.
