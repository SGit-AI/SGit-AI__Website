**At the foot of every article there is now a reader card.** Three scales, each starting in the middle:

- **How useful was this?** sets what you pay: 1 is free, 3 is the price, 5 is double.
- **More like this?** changes how often you see pages like it in [your newsroom](/account/newsroom.html).
- **Level of detail** tells your persona whether you want it lighter or deeper.
- **Every article you open joins your reading list**, and "don't charge me" is still there, at the end, as the exception. [Why](/articles/going-live-with-the-reading-meter.html).
