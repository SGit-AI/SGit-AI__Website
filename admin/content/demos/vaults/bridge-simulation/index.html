The one idea

A local story kept as a graph is worth something every day it matters, not only on the days it is news. The value is getting a disputed fact right and keeping it right; the readers use it through Reader Skills that answer their own situation; and because every claim names its evidence, every payment can walk back to the people who found it. The argument is in the article, The bridge, followed to the end, and the architecture in Story vault underneath, Reader Skills on top.
