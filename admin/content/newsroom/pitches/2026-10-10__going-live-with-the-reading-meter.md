The meter now charges real money by how much of a page is read, and every reader sees its balance in the top bar. The article that explains why, and writes down the numbers we will be judged on, should be where a reader lands when they wonder what that number is. It supersedes the open pitch for a meter in the browser, which describes the demonstration; its companion, who will game the reading meter, belongs beside it as a highlight.
