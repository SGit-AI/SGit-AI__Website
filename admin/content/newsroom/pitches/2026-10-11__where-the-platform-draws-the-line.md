Microsoft made Execution Containers generally available on 7 October, the most complete platform control for agents so far. This article is both a briefing (how MXC and the Agent Control Specification actually work, with a policy that validates against the 1.0.0 schema) and the positioning answer to "does Microsoft make RiskMandate unnecessary?": the platform draws its line at paths, addresses and tool calls, and the business logic above it is the deployer's. Eight scenarios run three ways make the split concrete. It is the practical companion to the two Nadella articles and belongs beside them; it would also fit the "Behaviour policy in practice" collection.
