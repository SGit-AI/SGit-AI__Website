Satya Nadella published "Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era" on 10 October, and it argues, from a model vendor's side, what this site has argued since The ultimate insider: treat models as insiders, keep authority outside them, constrain them so you can trust them. This article translates his post idea by idea into our vocabulary (the grant is not the mandate, the enforcer test, every risk is already accepted) and names the two places the languages differ. It is a next-day answer to a widely read post, and it ties the "Behaviour policy in practice" and "How agents decide" collections together. Suggested: lead with this one, and put its companion, authority-outside-the-model (the seven principles one by one, with an honest maturity table), alongside as a highlight.
