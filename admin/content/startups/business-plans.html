Business plans to build on sgit

These are businesses we think should exist on top of sgit and the work published on this site, written up in enough detail for somebody else to start one. Each plan is a vault: the plan as an app and as documents, the numbers marked as hypotheses, the facts with their sources, and prototypes enough to serve the first customer. We are looking for business partners to build them. Where nobody does, we may build one ourselves, depending on funding and on who is available. Either way the plan is public, on the same terms as the code.

Lesson Loop
24 September 2026		Capture what a padel coach knows at the end of every lesson, in a record the player holds: a voice memo, an agent, a lesson note, match notes from the player, and a two-minute briefing for the next coach, in any club or country. How to take one

  1. Open it. Every plan's page prints its public read key. Open it in the browser with nothing installed, or clone it with sgit clone.
  2. Read the one-page version. Every plan starts at plan/00-START-HERE.md, with a reading order after it.
  3. Test the numbers. Every price and every hour is a first guess, and every plan says so. The first ten customers are the test.
  4. Keep what you build. The plans are offered on the same terms as the code behind them: take it, run it, change it.
  5. Send back what broke. It is the one thing we ask, and it makes the next plan better.

The architecture and the serviceHow it is built on vaults, what the customer gets, and what the operator does not do.

Go-to-market and the first ninety daysWho buys first, the free thing that opens the door, and what "done" means at each stage.
Investors, risks and open questionsThe case for backing it, what could sink it, and what we do not know yet.
PrototypesEnough to serve the first customer: playbooks, specifications, set-up steps and worked examples.
PUBLIC.mdThe rules the vault is published under: nothing private, no write token, and an app that asks for no permissions.
