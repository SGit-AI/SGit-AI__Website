A proposed partnership between sgit.ai and DigitalOcean

DigitalOcean is the shortest path from "I want to try vaults" to a running server. Spaces is S3-compatible object storage with a built-in CDN, App Platform runs a container, and the DigitalOcean Marketplace explicitly invites open-source projects as 1-Click Apps. For the developers and small teams DigitalOcean serves, a one-click vault server is exactly the right size.

Nothing on this page is confidential, and there has been no conversation yet. Every statement about DigitalOcean comes from its own public pages, linked. Every statement about sgit points at the deployment documentation, a published vault or a page on this site. sgit is Apache-2.0, so DigitalOcean does not need our permission to run it or to build on it. A partnership is about doing that well and together. The general argument is on the cloud platforms page.

In short

  • Everything sgit needs, in the simplest form. Spaces for storage with a CDN, and App Platform for the container.
  • A 1-Click App is the natural listing. The DigitalOcean Marketplace invites open-source projects.
  • The audience fits. Developers and small teams who want encrypted, versioned data without running a platform.
  • What we would like: help publishing the 1-Click App, and a tested Spaces deployment.

Where we are with DigitalOcean today

We have not deployed on DigitalOcean yet. It is on this list because it is the platform where a developer is most likely to try something like sgit on a Saturday afternoon, and a 1-Click App is the fastest way to let them.
